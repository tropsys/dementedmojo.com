ABOUT

SO… WHAT IS DEMENTED MOJO?

Demented Mojo is a little bit music, a little bit projects, a little bit technology, and a whole lot of whatever seems interesting at the time.

One day it might be guitars and recording gear. The next could be a property project, AI, tools, a product review, or one of the dogs stealing the show.

The point isn’t to stay inside one niche. It’s to make things, learn things, share what works, laugh when it doesn’t, and see where it goes.
